# CosyWorld image prompts

These two concept images were generated with the built-in image tool.
The prompts below record the first visual language sheet and world atlas.
Character treatment, palette, and dream places are first-pass proposals.
The companion [style guide](style-guide.md) defines the reusable direction.

## Visual language sheet

Output: [visual-language.png](assets/visual-language.png)

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: CosyWorld visual language guide, first concept sheet, wide landscape 3:2.
Primary request: Develop an art direction sheet for an anthropomorphic anime world of whimsical cosmic horror, centered on a small cosy blue cottage tucked beneath the roots and canopy of an immense ancient living oak. The Tree of Dreams grows recursive dreams, each dream holding another dream, and feeds them to its physical body, the oak. Rati, Skull, and Whiskerwind are one mind with three distinct personalities.
Layout: an art director's elegant illustrated field guide on warm parchment. Upper 60% is one rich, large hero illustration of the oak and cottage at blue hour. Lower 40% is three clearly separated full character studies with their names underneath, plus a compact row of six large pigment swatches at the bottom. Make the title at top "COSYWORLD" with small subtitle "The dreaming oak". Use restrained readable serif typography.
Hero composition: low, wide, spreading oak silhouette with knotted bark and a vast soft canopy; tactile cottage of blue painted timber, crooked slate roof, stone hearth, small amber windows, kettle steam. Amber light spills onto moss and practical garden paths. In a few openings in the upper canopy, another violet and teal star universe is visible; one opening contains a distant version of the very same oak and cottage, and its tree contains a still smaller version. Preserve the human-scale warmth and the immense age of the tree. Domestic hospitality extended to an impossible scale; eerie beauty, gentle absurdity and cosmic awe coexist. One warm distant window repeats in the sky.
Character study 1 "Rati": an adult feminine mousefolk host with expressive anime eyes, rounded mouse ears, dark wavy hair, blue knitted scarf, practical apron and warm ochre clothing; holding a ceramic teacup; brisk, capable, amused expression. Clear mouse features and paws, self-possessed adult proportions.
Character study 2 "Skull": a large charcoal wolf with a silver muzzle, deep chest, strong quiet silhouette, slightly lowered head and dry attentive gaze; four paws, physically grounded at rest; small amber reflected light in the eyes. A silent wolf with a vivid personality conveyed through posture.
Character study 3 "Whiskerwind": a small golden catlike wind creature, bright clear eyes, curling whiskers, tail that curls into a breeze; expressive upright posture. Above it float exactly three expressive pictographs: a leaf, a moon, and a teacup. Its speech is exclusively emojis.
Shared style: painterly fantasy illustration, confident selective anime linework, expressive restrained faces, carefully observed fur and wood, beautiful soft edges in foliage, crisp silhouettes around eyes and hands, visibly painted gouache-like surfaces, authored asymmetry. The three characters feel drawn by one artist and belong together with the environment.
Palette: hearth honey #E6AD60, worn oak #71513C, moss #617755, night indigo #243653, dream violet #9566D6, star mint #73D9CB; warm paper #F2E7D2. Quiet earth colors dominate, star colors concentrated in impossible details. The swatches are simple paint rectangles with these short labels: Hearth, Oak, Moss, Night, Dream, Star.
Image contents consist of painted art, the stated title, character names and six color labels. Warmth, personality and legible recurring forms carry the design.
```

## Land and dream atlas

Output: [world-map.png](assets/world-map.png)

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: CosyWorld illustrated world map, first art direction proposal, wide landscape aspect 3:2, high detail, readable place labels.
Primary request: Paint a beautiful fantasy atlas of a whimsical cosmic horror world with an anthropomorphic anime soul. The central landmark is a real old oak and a little blue cottage beneath it. The oak is the physical body of the Tree of Dreams, which creates dreams containing further dreams and feeds them back to the physical oak. The land and the dreams are two layers of one illustration.
Composition: an elevated three-quarter illustrated map of a small wooded country. Warm parchment edge, restrained serif labels, hand-painted terrain, tiny warm windows, practical footpaths. Lower central 65% shows the waking land. Upper 25% reveals the Tree of Dreams through the oak canopy: recursive violet-and-teal nested branching windows, each with a smaller woodland and distant oak inside. At the bottom a narrow cutaway shows roots curling into nested rings of old dreams. Keep generous spacing between the labels. Keep the cottage and oak as the emotional and geographic center.
Title at top left exactly "COSYWORLD". Small subtitle "Land and dream atlas". Small footer "First map proposal".
Landmarks and labels:
Center: a very broad, ancient natural oak with a warm blue timber cottage tucked at its base, marked "Old Oak" and "Cosy Cottage" beside their respective landmarks.
Near west: cosy roadside inn with four lit windows, labeled "Mossbell Inn".
Near east: round cultivated garden with broad wet leaves, labeled "Rain-Soft Garden".
East beyond garden: silver winding path, labeled "Moonlit Trail".
Northeast of oak: maze-like beautiful forest repeating the same fork, labeled "Lost Woods".
At the north edge of Lost Woods: a quiet grey abbey and a flower meadow, one shared label "Abbey and Meadow".
Beyond Lost Woods toward far upper right: a crooked mansion with an impossibly long row of warm windows, labeled "Haunted Mansion".
Far east: large warmly lit library with a few shelves extending impossibly into the distance, labeled "Great Library".
Upper right beyond the forest: pine foothills, mountain switchbacks, lunar standing stones and a sunlit celestial temple above clouds, grouped label "High Country".
Lower right: reeds, jungle coast, indigo ocean and a faint underwater pearl-lit vertical descent, grouped label "Tide Country".
Far right edge by the library: luminous green and teal rectangular gateways mixed with real tree roots, labeled "Digital Realm".
Upper canopy dream layer: "Tree of Dreams". Tiny repeated oak-and-cottage windows show dreams inside dreams. Give them open loops and visible branching patterns.
Lower root cutaway: "Root Archive".
Cartographic language: earth paths are fine continuous warm brown lines. The dream branches are pale mint and violet organic branching light. Keep the main road west from inn to cottage, then east through garden and moonlit trail; fork north from garden to oak; fork from oak northeast to meadow and east into Lost Woods. Fine branch routes from Moonlit Trail reach the library and High Country; coast route from library passes marsh and jungle to ocean. The dream canopy connects visually to the central oak, and one fine root loop curls back beneath the cottage.
Style: painterly illustrated atlas with expressive anime environmental design, thoughtful gouache texture, precise small architecture and botanical detail, clear silhouette and scale hierarchy. Hearth honey #E6AD60, worn oak #71513C, moss #617755, night indigo #243653, dream violet #9566D6, star mint #73D9CB on warm paper. A limited number of uncanny details carry the scale: a distant ocean curve resembles a closed eye, the mansion has too many evenly lit windows, the oak repeats inside its own canopy. Hospitality and vast cosmic strangeness coexist. Show a warm window even within the deepest sea area.
All lettering uses only the exact labels above and short title/footer text. Make a functional, spacious, beautiful map that an illustrator and world designer can use as a first visual proposal.
```
