# Speech after a play

The first ordinary Wait speaks once per visit. `actors[id].waitSpeech` saves its place and turn through reload and a short log. Movement resets it. Older saves use retained visit history.

Item actions, successful exchanges, choices, course and travel give the avatar a reaction. Authored echoes, talk, refusals, battles, meetings and long rests keep their own speech rules.

Shared play fixes these voices at commit. A new bundle preserves replay; released bundles keep their bytes. Private thoughts keep their six-turn timing and can follow a quiet Wait.
