# The City Gate and the road to Athens

The City Gate is the junction outside Thebes. The battlefield and Cithaeron
road meet there. A posted guard controls entry to the courtyard; people
inside can leave. The road continues to Eleusis, where speaking with the
mothers begins their own request for passage to Athens. The ship supplies
another way home through the Theban harbor.

An optional `oneWay` field on an existing guardable edge limits the standing
guard to its authored direction. The edge still supplies both walking exits.
Other guardable edges keep their existing two-way rule.

Greek revision 24 preserves people and items on Cithaeron. Saved road
knowledge is rebuilt through the current junction, old removed route latches
are retired, and the guard takes the courtyard entrance on reload. The
released shared bundles preserve earlier journals.

Validation covers real movement from the courtyard and battlefield while the
guard is posted, independent arrival at Eleusis, the Athens and ship return
loop, old saves, and local/shared agreement.
