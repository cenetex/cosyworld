# Emergent play in the Greek world

The 44 plays supply the people, places, obligations, disputes, and memories of one Greek world. A household keeps vigil, a watch guards its gate, and a port stocks boats even when nobody takes part in the story those people came from. Players change that world by moving its goods, bringing people together, and putting credible information in the hands of people who can act on it. A run can have a satisfying journey while leaving most of the plays’ events untouched.

Revision 48 compiles 256 choices and 150 item records. Of the 35 plays that use the standard encounter template, 31 use a local object as the input to two Give outcomes. The proposal replaces that structure with ordinary services and pressures that share rules across the map. It uses existing cards and actions, with no dialogue menus, new verbs, or play-completion checklist. ## Revision 49

The playable implementation starts with Attica and extends into the Argolid, Marathon, Troezen, Pherae, the Trojan camp, and Sicily. Thaleia and the Danaids can bake the same loaf when it has run out. Lampito and the servant at Pherae can spend it on hospitality. Praxagora can set out the loaf and record who provided it. A meal keeps the Acropolis audience open for eight turns. The Treasury Key follows its real custody and the formal Acharnae orders still require their own proof.

Marathon provides blanket aid before the battle. At Troezen an actual conversation gives a traveler grounds to request the nurse’s hearing while the sealed accusation remains on the steps. The Areopagus can act on eligible Aeschylean evidence before the trial. The Trojan watch can record both reports over a day and night without choosing an official account. Polyphemus can recognize ordinary Wine brought from Thebes. The source index below also includes proposals for the rest of the pack. ## The shared rules

| What a player can do now                                                                                          | What it should change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Use or move a supply.** Take, carry, lay, eat, or give actual goods.                                            | Use the existing Bread, Water, Wine, Olive Oil, and Travel Provisions where they fit. A loaf served at a meeting is the same loaf the traveler could have eaten or used for a voyage. Spend or transfer the item through its normal lifecycle. The next traveler sees its real location or the next chance to get it. |
| **Learn or establish a fact.** Read a record in place, hear a person who knows it, or be present when it happens. | A record proves what was recorded. A witness proves what they saw. A named original matters when authenticity, secrecy, or delivery has a real effect. The venue and the authority receiving proof decide what that proof enables.                                                                                    |
| **Change who is present.** Walk, accompany someone who agrees, wait, or return during an existing routine.        | A witness can reach a hearing, a petitioner can reach a keeper, and a party can remain at a meeting. The meeting has to happen with the required people present. A late traveler can read the result or ask someone who knows it.                                                                                     |

Give people standing duties, useful services, and the authority to respond to specific circumstances. Each Talk returns the response for the current circumstances. Players can ask again after the state changes. When incompatible responses are both possible, the person explains the stakes until a deliberate act gives one response clear grounds. The engine currently takes the first eligible consequential Talk and chooses between eligible Wait effects by seed. Use those paths only when the result follows from current authority and custody or the player is observing an ordinary routine. [Talk and Wait dispatch](../../v2/card-game/pack-registry.mjs#L849) · [World state and sourceable evidence](greek-drama-world.md#what-the-world-records)

Supplies buy time, create hospitality, and support travel. Political decisions require the consent of the people who can make them. Keep custody of an original when moving or opening it changes the story. A ballot pebble or watch tally can live at its venue as a fixture, with named people recording what was done there.

Use one item for each ordinary good, including a single Bread shared across suppliers. Review each portable letter and token by asking who could receive it, what their authority is, and what changes if they hold the original. Keep the Letter to Argos because its delivery to Electra can establish a fact. Keep formal orders and the like where possession has a legal effect. Move generic messages to the people who can tell them. Make durable, public procedural information readable at its venue. Ownership and content still have to agree in older saves.

Use the existing turn, daylight, dwelling, and supply rules for timing. Tell the player what waits for them and what may move on. Let a missed appointment lead to a return visit, another eligible contact, or a posted notice that gives a viable next move. An unattended situation can change availability. A sovereign commitment, expulsion, or final verdict needs an authorized, deliberate act.

## Research and review standards

- **Prepare a situation.** Justin Alexander describes preparing people, initial circumstances, and goals that survive player choices. Merge overlapping pressures into the same world routines. Several leads can tell a player how a hearing works, while the hearing can still require one authentic original. [Don’t Prep Plots](https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/4147/roleplaying-games/dont-prep-plots) · [Three Clue Rule](https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/1118/roleplaying-games/three-clue-rule)
- **Expose the stakes.** Cairn’s Warden principles give players useful information and warn them of danger before they decide. Chris McDowall assesses decisions through information, choice, and impact. Here a card, sign, or ordinary reply should identify which supply will be spent, who will stand surety, or what an act makes public. Its effect should be visible on the map, at the table, or in the next offer. [Cairn Warden principles](https://cairnrpg.com/second-edition/players-guide/overview-and-principles/) · [Information, Choice, Impact](https://www.bastionland.com/2018/09/the-ici-doctrine-information-choice.html)
- **Let a few rules interact.** Harvey Smith’s systemic level-design examples make resources valuable across multiple situations because each system obeys legible rules. Warren Spector describes how a sequence of smaller player decisions becomes a story. Use goods with competing uses and effects a second player can see. Keep resources scarce in the world and broad in what they can do. [Harvey Smith on systemic level design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/marketing/conversations-from-gdc-europe-bill-fulton-zeno-colaco-harvey-smith) · [Warren Spector’s Deus Ex postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/postmortem-ion-storm-s-i-deus-ex-i-)
- **Make time and recovery legible.** Cairn’s exploration procedures attach consequences to time spent. Here travel and waiting already spend turns, so reuse them to create an opportunity cost. For an expected change, name the local person who will act, what they will do, and how the player can find out or return. ## First replay through Athens and Piraeus

The first slice connects Acharnae, the Acropolis, and Piraeus. Start with Thaleia’s existing Water-for-Bread exchange. Today it requires the public Acharnae allotment to have been issued. Make ordinary baking available when the loaf is used up, Water is given to Thaleia, and the quay has access to its normal supply. Keep the formal public allotment and the private order on Dicaeopolis’s credit as scoped benefits with their existing records. Even before either agreement, Thaleia can explain her stock and the next chance to get a loaf. The existing baker and the recipe at the harbor keep their roles. From the same starting state, one traveler can Give her the loaf when she can serve the participants. Her hospitality extends the time they can stay and spends the loaf. A sailor needs another loaf before the shipmaster can make Travel Provisions from Bread, Figs, and Wine at the harbor. Another traveler can keep the loaf for the ship and visit the Acropolis empty-handed. A meeting can still happen when the parties are present, and they decide what they agree to. The Treasury Key controls the chest through its own custody in either run. A traveler arriving later can ask Lampito how the meeting went or ask Thaleia what is available. If a public Acharnae order exists, they can learn that specific fact from its record and use it at the quay under the normal terms. In a third run, skip Acharnae’s settlement and the Acropolis negotiations. Ordinary trade, stock recovery, and travel should still produce a full trip. Compare what goods exist, who is available, what a player can actually do, and what they can honestly cite on return.

## Independent review decisions

Two independent reviewers checked all four teams’ proposals against OSR practice and the actual pack rules. Both found the same risk in different parts of the map. Moving a resolution from Give to a required Read, Wait, and Talk sequence preserves the fixed route through a play. They recommended keeping the admission rules, standing services, and disputed custody, then letting any combination of eligible actions bring them into play. [Cairn Warden principles](https://cairnrpg.com/second-edition/players-guide/overview-and-principles/) · [Don’t Prep Plots](https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/4147/roleplaying-games/dont-prep-plots)

- **Availability is shared, consent is specific.** Standing shelter in Marathon and ordinary work on the shores exist before any source event. A real bond may change custody, and a recognized passage mark may enable a particular journey. An idle wait or a first visit cannot by itself commit a disputed irreversible act.
- **A clue explains the next action.** The Guard’s Summons describes a custody decision after it happens. Demophon or a public procedure can explain the requirements earlier. Personal knowledge can come from reading or conversation, so a traveler able to repeat a claim cannot automatically count as an eyewitness. An authentic original and a named witness must carry their own evidence. [Rule predicates](../../v2/card-game/pack-rules.mjs#L151) · [Claim provenance](../../v2/card-game/claims.mjs#L164)
- **Spend supplies where they actually help.** A loaf buys hospitality, with no implied vote or right to speak for the treasury. A debt, sanctuary guarantee, or hearing responds to its own authority. Move portable goods through existing custody and recovery, and leave scenic badges, cups, and tallies at their venues.
- **Use the exact receiving authority.** Thaleia can recognize the existing Acharnae pledges, and Electra can report a Tauris delivery made to her. These give neither office general power over a different historical account. Keep a private meeting’s details private and make public facts findable through an actual witness or authorized record.

## Implementation and playtest gates

Use the Attic circuit to test repeatable services, Troezen and the sanctuary to test evidence and refuge, and the shores to test cargo and safe returns. Keep saved outcome and receipt IDs readable as bindings change. Revision 49 keeps the 256-choice limit for active play and gives archived entries a separate limit so earlier saves can still replay their history. Expand only once the same rules can sustain different kinds of play. [Choice budget](../../v2/card-game/pack-schema.mjs#L357) · [Existing save recovery](../../v2/card-game/legacy-saves.mjs#L606)
