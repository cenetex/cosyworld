# Away avatars answer as residents

An avatar's account claim and its current world presence are different.
The server's journaled presence seats the controlled actors. A claimed avatar
whose player is away, or an available unclaimed avatar, already acts as a
resident. Locked/absent avatars remain absent. This does not infer presence
from browser logout.

Away avatars now use the ordinary ask, trade and combat decisions, including
refusal, item value, wants, capacity, sanctuary and combat restrictions. Direct
taking from their hands remains forbidden. Present humans remain protected:
the rules do not consent to trades, gifts or new challenges on their behalf.
Claims, inventories and quest reward receipts are not reset on departure or
return. Quest custody remains separate from interaction eligibility.

A Talk directed at an away avatar uses that avatar's resident action for the
turn. It stays for its reply instead of walking away before the committed
speech cast is formed. The speech authority still admits only the speakers
named by the committed play.

This slice does not change offstage movement or player leveling. Those use
separate scheduling and claimable reward contracts and need their own tests.
The source change requires a new immutable shared rules bundle before landing;
old bundles remain unchanged for journal replay.
