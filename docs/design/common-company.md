# Current company from a direct Talk

A direct Talk records current company on its existing conversation pair.
The optional `company` field names the latest real Talk event. The pair’s
first `seq` and `turn` remain the durable evidence for personal routes.

Separation, sleep, harm and disabled content clear current company during
the committed play. Reset already clears the actor’s conversation pairs.
Waits, quest claims and plays in other rooms keep a shared encounter while
the people stay together. There is no shared turn timer.

Earlier saves keep their first Talk evidence and start with no current
company marks. Reload preserves the marks; the rolling log may trim their
source after its identity has been saved. Life records and keepsakes keep
their existing rules. Invitation consent will use this common fact in the
next slice, alongside saved visits and each traveler’s route conditions.
