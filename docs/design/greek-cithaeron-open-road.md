# The Cithaeron road stays open

Greek pack revision 23 keeps the road from the Exposed Battlefield to
Cithaeron open at all times. Burial progress, writ custody, and the guard's
tablet do not control this geography. The separate road latches left by
revision 22 are no longer valid saved routes.

On upgrade, a revision 22 Greek save advances to 23 and drops only the obsolete
`greek-battlefield>greek-cithaeron` latch. Other valid opened routes, pack flags,
receipts, item holders, and funeral progress remain intact. Saves without burial
or gate progress can travel the road after loading. A lawfully buried old save
also retains its funeral evidence and its other open routes.

The Hollow route keeps its existing main behavior. This change does not alter
the shade conversation, burial sequence, or access to the Hollow.

Shared rules version 120 carries the pack migration while version 119 and all
earlier bundles remain fixed for journal replay.
