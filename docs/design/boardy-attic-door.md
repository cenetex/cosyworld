# Boardy's attic doorway

Historical revision3 design, superseded by [Station admission](station-first-open-exits.md).

The Station has a three-link limit. Boardy's doorway moves to Mirror Attic
so the requested RatiRadio staff entrance can join Station without displacing
the carriage. The card still starts at Station; its directions name the Attic.
The lamp opens the Pond–Attic route. Boardy's two-human gate stays unchanged.
The doorway change must land with the staff entrance: Station loses one
reserved link as it gains the other. A separate release could seed a human
home into the temporarily free slot, leaving that saved world over capacity
when the staff entrance arrives.

Boardy revision 3 retains both crossing IDs. Its migration expands learned
Station–Parlour routes through Pond and Attic, deduplicates the resulting
steps, then records revision 3. Revision 1 still takes the existing migration
to revision 2 first. Actors, possessions, crossing discovery, human home doors,
unrelated routes, item history and journal entries stay intact. Released
engines retain their original Station doorway for historical replay.

Tests cover the legal card-and-lamp walk, return with one human, exact save
preservation, repeated upgrades, old-engine route knowledge, and the C host.
The browser smoke follows the same walk at desktop and phone widths.
