# Greek story gifts

Talk with a person to hear their account. To choose which account to follow,
take the story item and give it to the person whose account you want. The
item describes what its possible recipients know or want.

| Story | Item | Give it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helen | Helen’s Account Tablet at the harbor | Myrto for Egypt or the Chorus Elder for Troy |
| Atreids | Record of the House of Atreus at the crossroads | Ismene for the tomb, Creon for the palace, or Dione for the farmhouse |
| Ajax | Teucer’s Written Plea at the Greek camp | Odysseus for a public burial, Tecmessa for a household burial, or Teucer for a witnessed appeal |

After hearing Helen or Electra, give the witness tablet to the person you
want to support. Menelaus needs provisions for the passage as well.

Talk with Myrto to learn the way to Ajax’s camp after Dione’s return. Give
Ajax’s shield to Tecmessa. Hear Teucer’s plea and read it with Odysseus before
offering it to a recipient. Set the tools and linen down at the grave. Wait
there for each step of the burial. The appeal needs Odysseus as a witness.
Talk with Teucer after the burial and give his signed plea to Dione.

The opening follows the Polynices rites. Give Antigone the oil, cloth and
shovel in order in the Hollow. The Helen, Atreid and Ajax stories are in the
full Greek scope. Saved decisions and recitals keep their existing keys.
