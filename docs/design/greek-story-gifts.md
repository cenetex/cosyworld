# Greek story gifts

Talk to hear an account. The story item names the people who want it.

| Story | Item | Give it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helen | Helen’s Account Tablet | Myrto for Egypt or the Chorus Elder for Troy |
| Atreids | Record of the House of Atreus | Ismene for the tomb, Creon for the palace, or Dione for the farmhouse |
| Ajax | Teucer’s Written Plea | Odysseus for a public burial, Tecmessa for a household burial, or Teucer for a witnessed appeal |

Give Antigone the oil, cloth and shovel in order. At Ajax’s grave, set down the tools and linen and Wait for the rite. Saved decisions and recitals keep their keys.
