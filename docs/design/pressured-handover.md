# Pressured handovers

Giving the exact demanded item to the challenger during an active threat
records a pressured handover after accepted custody actually changes.
The rules capture the defender, challenger and stake before the transfer.
The committed Give carries that context and says the item changed hands
under pressure. Ordinary gifts, trades and forced losses keep their meanings.

This context guides trust, company, keepsakes, helpful-gift memories and
incident repair. A pressured handover preserves those social records.
Quest completion and reward claims follow their existing rules.

New battles save the sequence of their actual committed Threaten event.
That sequence survives scene-log trimming and appears on the later handover.
Earlier saves may lack the opening source; their handover records a null
source sequence. The active demand still supports the transfer context;
later episode memory can distinguish the missing opening evidence.

Validation checks retained source events against the exact pair, stake,
place and opening turn. Released engines retain their recorded behavior.
