# Personal route prerequisites

A direct Talk saves its two participants, first event number and turn in
`conversations`. Each person keeps that fact through later quest draws and
shortened scene history. A save upgrade recovers retained Talk events. Resetting
an avatar clears its conversation facts. Retelling knowledge keeps its own
existing record.

Pack conditions may use `talked` with a person ID. Routes may add
`travelerRequires`, evaluated for the person entering. The world's ordinary
`requires`, live gates and latched gates still describe the physical route.
`personalRouteOpen` supplies the same personal check to party travel. Existing
routes keep their current behavior until pack data adds a personal condition.

The next slice gives a resident a reachable prerequisite concern while keeping
its main want. Then the Hollow can ask each traveller to speak with the shade.
Accepted funeral progress keeps its explicit compatibility path. The former
world latch remains a physical gate fact.
