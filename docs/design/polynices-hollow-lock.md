# Polynices’ Hollow opens through his shade

Greek pack revision 22 replaces the open Hollow exit with a sealed route. The
road to Cithaeron stays open. Each traveler must
have spoken directly with Polynices’ Shade before entering the Hollow. The
locked route shows that prerequisite without creating a quest card. The shade
appears at night on the battlefield.

A legal Talk by a controlled player records `greek-polynices-heard` through the
existing pack choice system. This opens the physical route. The personal Talk
condition still applies to each traveler after the shade leaves at dawn.
Antigone chooses the Hollow as her home after the shared event, then approaches
the shade to earn her own entry. Her route planner chooses ordinary Move and
Talk actions. Offstage Talk uses the same action recorder within the existing
resident turn budget. Her sleep schedule remains in force; a player can wake
her by speaking to her before visiting the shade. A Talk chosen on the last night turn checks the subject
and conditions at that chosen clock; its effects and receipt belong to the
committed turn, even as dawn arrives.

The pack's old `met` record means being in the same place, so it is distinct from
the committed Talk required here. NPC Talk uses the normal resident conversation
path; the pack's Talk choice belongs to controlled players.

Accepted Anoint, Veil or Bury flags also open the physical route. This preserves
progress in existing funeral saves. The return from the Hollow stays available.
Burial ends the shade's appearances. Someone who has not spoken to him before
then cannot gain new entry afterwards; funeral flags never invent a conversation.
The save upgrade advances the pack revision and retains accepted flags and
receipts.

The shared rules bundle is versioned after current main, with earlier bundles
kept byte-for-byte for replay. Source tests cover the lock, quest, Talk, resident
route, partial saves and the current shared bundle. Browser proof covers the
existing locked card and quest flow at desktop and phone widths.
