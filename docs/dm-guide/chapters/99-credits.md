# Credits and License {#credits}

## Edition {#edition}

**The CosyWorld Referee's Guide**, first working edition, 30 July 2026.

Written for the CosyWorld project from its product laws, accepted architecture
decisions, current runtime behavior, and groomed design targets. Edited to
distinguish Core Rules, Accepted Targets, Playtests, and Referee Practices.

## Illustrations and diagrams {#art-credits}

The five storybook raster illustrations were generated specifically for this
edition with OpenAI's built-in ImageGen tool, without source images. Their
prompts, review process, and file hashes are recorded in the source
publication's illustration provenance file.

The five explanatory diagrams are original SVG artwork built from live text
and simple shapes for clear print and accessible digital reading.

No proprietary Dungeon Master's Guide text or art is reproduced in this
book. Its procedures are original CosyWorld material informed by the broad
traditions of adventure-game refereeing and open rules references.
