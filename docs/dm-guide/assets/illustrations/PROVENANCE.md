## Reproduction briefs

Each image was a new generation, not an edit. These briefs combine the shared
direction above with the scene-specific instructions used for the final
artwork. Image generation is nondeterministic, so they document intent rather
than promising identical pixels.

- **Cover:** Vertical book-cover composition with a warm cottage and lantern
  in the lower foreground, a small stone cairn beside a branching trail, and a
  distant old ruin under a moonlit blue horizon. Leave calm open sky for title
  treatment. The image itself contains no lettering.
- **Hearth to frontier:** Wide interior-to-exterior scene. A varied group of
  small travelers calmly checks packs, shelter, map, and lantern at a warm
  shared hearth. An open doorway shows a cool green frontier path. Emphasize
  preparation, consent, companionship, and the contrast between sanctuary and
  chosen uncertainty.
- **Noticing a Lead:** Wide woodland scene with travelers kneeling beside
  connected physical clues: a clear track in mud, fibers caught on bark, bent
  reeds, and a route continuing toward a ridge. The figures are observant, not
  alarmed. Make the evidence legible without labels, arrows, or interface
  symbols.
- **Readable threshold:** Wide close view of an old cellar doorway with a
  plain keyhole, scrape marks in dust, a fine bell cord, slightly displaced
  stones, and warm light leaking from the far side. Show several fair sensory
  Tells and more than one plausible approach. No figure is harmed and no trap
  is triggered.
- **Return home:** Wide evening cottage gathering. Travelers set a recovered
  brass bell and a few silver leaves on a shared table while residents welcome
  them. Packs and muddy boots show the completed journey; lamplight and open
  expressions make the homecoming warm. The recovered objects remain small,
  physical, and believable.

These files contain no external art inputs. Their use remains subject to the
