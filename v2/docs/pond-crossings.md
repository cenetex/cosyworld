# Black Pond crossings

Forest and Wine-Dark Sea use the same compiled crossing vocabulary. Forest
owns its Steam Clock and bell window; the Greek bridge owns its lamp sequence.
No current engine rule names either pack or its destination.

A quiet pond and four resolved cover/take transitions complete the Greek
signal. `while` requires a lit, fueled lamp at the pond and no audible sound
at the pond or clearing. The route additionally requires uncovered light.
Snuffing, running dry, leaving or sound cancels the signal. Inspection,
reloads, generated speech and rejected actions never advance it. The harbor
return remains unconditional. Signal phase is internal, not displayed.

An open crossing supplies the painted ship layer, its scene-derived boarding
portrait and the Board verb together. The action is checked again when played.
The original empty pond, including the standing mirror, is unchanged outside
the ship/reflection mask. There are no intermediate visual progress markers.

The Forest source continues to own its two-ring opening, sound-gated return,
repeatable local clock action and resident temperament. Its physical mechanics
are preserved. Obsolete next-step/progress coaching is no longer displayed.

Schema-6 Steam Clock records migrate into schema-7 crossings/fixture sounds.
Greek histories, custody and memories are retained; old Greek games keep
full geography. New Greek games use the seven-place opening. The old lamp
phase migrates to shared crossing state without counting a new action.

Run the shared Forest, ship, Greek, image-first and Theban scenery suites.
Tests may examine internal phase; player interfaces must not expose it.
