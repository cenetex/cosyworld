Four older plays lead to work in the present Theban aftermath. Talk gives each
source its author and time. Each of the twelve deed cards ends on a physical
action. Four return cards bring a carried account to Dione.

After returning Dione's water and sharing the opening response with her, choose
**Follow the earlier Theban accounts** at her courtyard. The Guard tells Seven,
Melea keeps the two palace accounts, and Melitta tends the mountain path. Cithaeron
connects to the existing sanctuary path. Each later deed asks for the preceding
source fact and the earlier completed action. The copied objects rest on the
keepers' benches so a carrier can pick them up through the normal Take action.

Melea and Melitta are original present-day people. Historical speakers stay
inside their named accounts. In Euripides's Phoenician Women, Jocasta and Oedipus
live during the brothers' war. Sophocles's earlier inquiry and the existing
Colonus account keep their own histories. The existing Antigone, Colonus and
Argive mother-plea records and earned verses keep their identities and wording.

The family-first inquiry stays pending until the public reading. The gathering
agreement requires separate acceptances from Melitta and the Elder, followed by
placement of the same written terms. Their agreement covers Melitta's group and
the Elder's neighbors. Each named result has a short Talk fact at its witness.

For a return, the acting carrier must hold the account and know its completed
result. Another player can learn that fact from a witness or a later retelling.
The return records its own reader. A saved shared branch and a person's learned
facts keep their separate identities. The original actor keeps the earlier deed.
