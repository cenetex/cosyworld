    The reflected shore

    Play cards to guide Vale. Pip and the keeper take a turn after each play.

    Tap a card to raise it. Tap a second card to combine them. Tap a raised card to play. The card shows what will happen.

    You can also drag a card or raised pair into the scene. Drag one card onto another to form a pair. Tap the scene or press Escape to lower your cards.

    A card you meet for the first time is shown large, then joins your hand marked new. A location card travels there; the scene darkens and names the place before you arrive. The current location passes a turn. Pair a carried item with the current location to put it down, or with an avatar to give it.

    Each card play leaves memories. Avatars, items, and places remember what happens around them. Their words grow from those memories after a play.

    Pair an avatar with another location to ask them to accompany Vale. Tap either raised card to play the request. Their tastes, current interests, and remembered grievances shape their answer. An agreement takes both avatars and their items along that path. Each request takes one turn.

    Oil flask + Lamp adds fuel. Black cloth + Lamp blocks light. Black cloth + Bell muffles sound. Play an avatar card to hear what they want.

    Each avatar card shows a target hand: items to hold, a place, and company. Complete a quest program to gain a level. New programs grow from the world. Personal tastes and memories shape bargains and company. Each new meeting brings another person’s tastes and wants into play. Your choices shape their shared story.

    Pair one of your items with an item held by another avatar to trade. Their tastes and feelings about you shape the price. The card shows both sides of the exchange. Pair an item with an avatar to give it as a gift.

    The cast remembers noise, spoiled light, threats, and gifts. A useful gift can soften a grievance. Read their cards for the current mood.

    Tap a speaking line to reveal it. Tap again to continue. Each exchange also advances at a reading pace. The hand rests until the exchange finishes. The arrows let you read earlier lines.

    At Black pond, ring the uncovered Bell twice in a row to reach the Steam Clock. The clearing has a path back to the pond.
