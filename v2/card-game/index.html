The reflected shore

Play cards to guide Vale. Pip and the keeper take a turn after each play.

Tap a card to raise it. Tap a second card to combine them. Tap a raised card to play. The card shows what will happen.

You can also drag a card or raised pair into the scene. Drag one card onto another to form a pair. Tap the scene or press Escape to lower your cards.

A location card travels there. The current location passes a turn. Pair a carried item with the current location to put it down, or with an avatar to give it.

Raise a card to read its latest words. Avatars, items, and places remember what happens around them. Their words grow from those memories after a play.

Oil flask + Lamp adds fuel. Black cloth + Lamp blocks light. Black cloth + Bell muffles sound. Play an avatar card to hear what they want.

Each avatar card shows a target hand: items to hold, a place, and people to meet. Every part must be true together. Completing a whole quest program earns a level. New programs grow from the items and people in the world. The cast pursues its own hands.

Pair one of your items with an item held by another avatar to trade. Their current hand decides what they keep and what they accept. The card shows both sides of the exchange. Pair an item with an avatar to give it as a gift.

Complete any listed choice to finish that step. The cast can find a new purpose when a bargain stalls.

Tap a speaking line to reveal it. Tap again to continue. The arrows let you read earlier lines.
