# Full cast: Strike, Hit and Guard

All 150 new source paintings are complete: 147 transparent portrait sprites and three opaque Oak backdrop alternates. They cover 56 avatar IDs through 50 canonical identities. Strike uses the existing `-attack.webp` filename convention.

Each row starts with the existing reference, followed by the three edits. Click any thumbnail for its full source. The previews use the official 512-pixel card variants. The [handoff](cast-README.md), [exact prompts and revisions](cast-art-prompts.json), and [reference hashes, generation IDs and encoding checks](cast-provenance.json) describe the complete delivery.

The original eight human wardrobes, Pip and Keeper remain in the [original combat set](README.md) and [Guard set](guard-README.md). Current rules still decide who may enter a standoff. Oak remains a place painting; these three scene transformations are available for a future backdrop presentation.
