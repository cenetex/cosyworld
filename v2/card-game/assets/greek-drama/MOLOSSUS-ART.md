# Molossus child portrait for Book IV

`assets/greek-drama/book-four-molossus.png` binds to
`greek-book-iv-andromache-molossus`. The Book IV owner confirmed the filename,
actor ID, age of about seven, clothes and shared portrait baseline.

Molossus has a calm wary child face, a simple complete linen household tunic,
a soft wool cloak, sandals and readable free hands. His whole figure and both
feet fit in the native 1024 by 1536 canvas. Transparent space above the child
keeps him at child height beside the adult cast. His visible figure occupies
about 65.4 percent of the canvas height.

The built-in image generation tool made both native images. The selected PNG
keeps its complete generated pixels and transparent alpha. The first native
portrait remains in `molossus-sources/initial.png`.
`molossus-art-provenance.json` records both complete prompts, source/reference
hashes, the source role, alpha bounds, the child height, companion references,
review sheets and browser screenshots.
