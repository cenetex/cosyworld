# Book III scenery

The built-in imagegen tool made these original paintings on October 28, 2026.
The source PNGs are kept in `book-three-sources/`. The WebP files retain their
full composition. The ordinary image compiler makes the card versions.
`book-three-scenery-provenance.json` records dimensions and source hashes.

## Lemnos day prompt

Use case: historical scene. Paint a wide 16:9 CosyWorld backdrop. Use
`ajax-camp-day.webp` as a style reference for warm stone, crisp painterly detail
and depth. Show a deserted ancient Greek island: a low rocky cave at the left,
a folded rough sleeping cloth and plain water basin inside, a clear foreground
for character portraits, and a footpath toward a sheltered stone landing at
the right. The berth and blue Aegean water are empty. A dry rocky headland,
sparse olive branches and low herbs frame the sunny afternoon. Keep the
middle readable in a phone crop. The painting is pure scenery.

## Lemnos night prompt

Use case: lighting and weather. Edit the day painting to clear moonlight.
Preserve the cave, cloth, bowl, rocks, footpath, landing, headland and complete
composition. Use a deep blue sky, small stars, a moon above the right-hand sea,
silver water and blue foreground light. A small oil lamp inside the cave lights
the cloth and bowl. The island, landing, berth and water stay empty. Keep the
scene readable behind game characters and in a phone crop.
