# Book II cast: second set

These six full-body portraits follow the Heracles and Women of Trachis roles
in `cosyworld-book-ii-design.md`. Their clothes use ordinary Greek linen and
wool in the approved painted cast style.

Megara has a plain linen dress, deep green mantle, and a clear speaking pose.
Amphitryon is an elderly man with white hair, a close beard, and a wooden staff.
Deianira has a slate-blue dress and a small folded wool cloth. Iole is an adult
woman in a plain sand-colored travel dress. Hyllus is Heracles's young grown
son, with a plain chiton and a family resemblance in his hair and brow.
Lichas is an adult courier with a small sealed parcel. Their faces, hair,
clothes, and gestures are distinct.

The approved `hero-heracles.png` from PR #1271 and existing Greek portraits
serve as style references. Each new PNG keeps the original generated pixels
and native transparent alpha. `book-two-cast-provenance.json` records the full
prompts, source-plan hash, roles, reference sources and hashes, generation names,
dimensions, alpha measurements, output hashes, and review backgrounds.

## Review

All six portraits are 1024 × 1536 pixels. Each saved PNG matches its generated
source byte for byte. Source and reference hashes, native clear alpha, and
visible margins were checked. Full heads, hands, hems, feet, the staff,
folded cloth, and sealed parcel fit within their original canvases.

Three browser sheets show Megara and Amphitryon over the palace stages,
Deianira and Iole over Trachis, and Hyllus and Lichas over Trachis.
Each sheet includes day and night views at desktop width and in 390 × 580
phone crops. The cast edges and identifying objects stay clear in both lights.
