# Melea and Melitta

These two full-body portraits serve the original Book I characters in the
Theban care and remembrance strands.

| File | Actor | Place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `book-one-melea.png` | `greek-melea`, Melea, keeper of accounts | `greek-palace` |
| `book-one-melitta.png` | `greek-melitta`, Melitta, mountain keeper | `greek-cithaeron` |

Melea keeps copied records and household provisions. She has a small wax tablet,
a plain woven linen dress, a muted blue-grey shawl, and a clear speaking gesture.
Melitta keeps the herdsman's account and speaks for her present worshipping
group. She has an everyday wool robe, an earthy wrap, and a leafy ivy branch.
Their faces, hair, clothes, and held objects are distinct.

The source roles come from `cosyworld-book-one-source-plan.json`. The provenance
file preserves that plan's source commit, hash, and the two role records.
The chapter owner will bind the exact actor and asset paths above.

The PNG files preserve the full native pixels and alpha channels from the
built-in image generation tool. `book-one-portrait-provenance.json` records
the full prompts, cast references, generation names, sizes, hashes, alpha
measurements, and review backgrounds.

## Review

Both portraits are 1024 × 1536 pixels. The saved PNGs match the generated
sources byte for byte. Their alpha channels have clear pixels, and their
visible heads, hands, hems, feet, tablet, and branch have clear margins.

Melea was reviewed over the day and night palace paintings. Melitta was
reviewed over the approved day and night Cithaeron paintings from PR #1269.
Each browser review sheet shows a full stage and a 390 × 580 phone crop.
Both portraits remain clear over their light and dark settings.
