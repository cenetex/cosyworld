# Book II art: first set

These nine images follow `cosyworld-book-ii-design.md` and the current
Pherae and Trachis place records. They use the existing painted Greek style.

## Places

| Place | Day | Night |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `greek-pherae` | `book-two-pherae-day.webp` | `book-two-pherae-night.webp` |
| `greek-trachis` | `book-two-trachis-day.webp` | `book-two-trachis-night.webp` |

Pherae has a quiet palace portico, an empty wooden chair by the inner door,
and a broad court for the cast. The plain and mountain ridge sit beyond one
side of the palace. Trachis has a low household facade, a central doorway,
messenger's sandals, and a workbench with folded wool. A side path leads toward
the wooded slopes. Each night view keeps its day view's layout.

quiet closed-mouth expression. Chloris has a grey-brown dress, plain apron,
and auburn braid. Heracles has a linen chiton, lion hide, and a club at rest.
The shared Heracles image serves separately named account identities in data.

## Sources and review

The four stages are 1672 × 941 pixels. Their original PNGs are in
`book-two-sources/`. The WebPs keep the full source size and use quality 94.
The five full-body portraits are 1024 × 1536 pixels. Their PNGs keep the
original generated pixels and native alpha channels. Each native PNG matches
its generated source byte for byte.

`book-two-art-provenance.json` records the source-plan hash, role notes,
every full prompt, cast and scenery references, generation names, sizes,
source and output hashes, alpha measurements, and review backgrounds.
The built-in image generation tool made every image and night edit.

Three browser sheets cover the Argive cast, Pherae, and Trachis. They show
day and night overlays at desktop width and in 390 × 580 phone crops.
The two new stages also have clear phone scenery views. Visible heads,
hands, feet, hems, the lion hide, and the club have clear native margins.
The cast edges remain clear over both light settings.

The Book II source owner will bind these exact paths. Chapter integration
owns optimized variants, public asset generation, shared rules, and the final
release version.
