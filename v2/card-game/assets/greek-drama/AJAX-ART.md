# Ajax chapter art

These six images cover the Greek camp at Troy after Ajax's death.
The camp has room for the cast in the foreground. Its distant fleet belongs
to the scenery. The shared travel ship keeps its own saved dock.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `ajax-camp-day.webp` | Camp stage in daylight |
| `ajax-camp-night.webp` | The same camp at night |
| `ajax-tecmessa.png` | Tecmessa, alone, with a clear background |
| `ajax-teucer.png` | Teucer with his bow at rest, with a clear background |
| `ajax-odysseus.png` | Odysseus in a speaking pose, with a clear background |
| `ajax-shield.png` | Ajax's shield, with a clear background |

The camp paintings are 1672 × 941 pixels. The three full-body portraits are
1024 × 1536 pixels. The shield is 1254 × 1254 pixels.

The original camp PNGs are in `ajax-sources/`. The portrait and shield files
are the original generated PNGs, including their alpha channels. Each saved
PNG matches the generated source byte for byte. The camp WebPs keep the full
source size and use quality 94.

`ajax-art-provenance.json` records the full prompts, style references, generation
names, sizes, hashes, processing, and alpha measurements. The built-in image
generation tool made every image. The night painting is an edit of the day
painting and keeps the same camp layout.

## Review

The day and night paintings were reviewed at full width and in a phone crop.
The three portraits and the shield were reviewed as browser overlays over both
paintings. The visible subjects have a clear margin around their edges.
The source copies, output sizes, alpha channels, and recorded hashes were checked.

The chapter integration owns the catalog paths, daylight registration, generated
image variants, public asset registration, shared rules, and release versions.
