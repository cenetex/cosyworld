# Helen chapter art

These four images cover Proteus's tomb in Egypt and two members of his
household for the `euripides-helen` strand. The place is `greek-egypt-tomb`.
The chapter plan is in `docs/worldpacks/greek-epic-loom/euripides.md`.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `helen-egypt-tomb-day.webp` | Proteus's tomb beside the Egyptian coast in daylight |
| `helen-egypt-tomb-night.webp` | The same tomb and court at night |
| `helen-theonoe.png` | Theonoe, a full-body portrait with a clear background |
| `helen-theoclymenus.png` | Theoclymenus, a full-body portrait with a clear background |

The tomb is the focus of a coastal palace court. Lotus columns, date palms,
and a low, flat shore give the setting its own character. The open foreground
has room for the cast. The night painting keeps the same layout.

The existing `chthonic-helen.png` and `chthonic-menelaus.png` portraits provide
Helen and Menelaus. They also serve as style references for the new portraits.
Theonoe has a light veil and teal shawl. Theoclymenus has an ochre mantle and
gold collar. Their poses support a conversation in the court.

The original stage PNGs are kept in `helen-sources/`, including the first
day painting and its coastal-horizon edit. The portrait PNGs preserve their
original pixels and alpha channels. The stage WebPs keep the full source size
and use quality 94.

`helen-art-provenance.json` records the prompts, references, generation names,
sizes, hashes, processing, and alpha measurements. The built-in image generation
tool made the images and the edits.

## Review

The two stages are 1672 × 941 pixels. Both full-body portraits are
1024 × 1536 pixels. Their visible subjects have clear margins at each edge.
The saved source PNGs match the generated files byte for byte. Source,
reference, and runtime image hashes were checked.

A browser review sheet shows Helen, Menelaus, Theonoe, and Theoclymenus over
both paintings at full width. It also shows the two new portraits over both
paintings in a 390-pixel phone crop. The tomb stays visible and the portrait
edges stay clear.

image variants, public asset registration, shared rules, and release versions.
