# Book III portraits: Rhesus and the Thracian court

Four full-body adult portraits follow the Book III source plan.

| File | Planned actor binding |
| --- | --- |
| `book-three-hector.png` | `greek-rhesus-hector` |
| `book-three-rhesus.png` | `greek-rhesus-rhesus` |
| `book-three-charioteer.png` | `greek-rhesus-charioteer` |
| `book-three-polymestor.png` | `greek-thrace-polymestor` |

Hector and Rhesus are distinct adult commanders with clear faces. Their
clothes and bronze armor follow the approved painted Greek style. The
charioteer wears ordinary travel clothes and a clean linen forearm wrap.
Polymestor is an older court host in the earlier hearing frame. Each image
shows one living adult with empty hands. Wounds, deaths and later blinding
remain in the attributed spoken account.

The built-in image generation tool made every portrait. The PNG files keep
their original generated pixels and native transparent alpha.
`book-three-rhesus-provenance.json` records full prompts, source-plan hashes,
actor bindings, style references and hashes, generated source names,
dimensions, source and output hashes, alpha measurements and review sheets.

## Review and handoff

All four portraits are 1024 by 1536 pixels. Source and output hashes match
byte for byte. Native clear alpha and visible margins are checked. Full
heads, hands, hems and both feet fit within each original canvas.

Three browser sheets show the two commanders, the charioteer and Polymestor.
Each sheet has day and night overlays at desktop width and in a 390 by 580
phone view. Approved Greek camp and court paintings provide the review light
settings. Chapter stage bindings belong to the source owner.

This art draft targets `codex/greek-book-iii`. The chapter owner will bind the
four exact paths and native left-facing metadata, build optimized variants,
update public assets and run full game checks with the final chapter source.
The separate bandage and testimony items retain their chapter identities.
