# House of Blood: palace and farmhouse witnesses

Six adult portraits cover the palace, farmhouse and refuge witnesses. Chrysothemis and the tutor belong to Sophocles's account. The farmer and old servant belong to Euripides's farmhouse account. Hermione and the Phrygian servant keep their own speech and present choices. Each figure has a distinct face and empty hands.

passed their focused checks. Full heads, hands, hems and both feet fit within each
native canvas. The review sheets show both light settings at desktop width and
