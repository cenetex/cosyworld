# House of Blood: Delphi, court and Tauris portraits

Six portrait assets cover Delphi, Ares Hill and Tauris. The Furies and Captive Women each use one compact cutout of three adult women with distinct faces and readable hands. The priestess is older, Athena wears her helmet and aegis, and Thoas is a mature coastal king. Iphigenia keeps her approved Aulis facial landmarks in mature priestess clothes.

All portraits are native 1024 by 1536 PNGs. The built-in image generation tool
made each image. Every PNG preserves its generated pixels and native transparent
alpha. Full prompts, the source queue, reference commits and hashes, source/output
hashes, alpha bounds and review-sheet hashes are in `sacred-cast-provenance.json`.

## Review and handoff

Source copies, queue bindings, reference commits, clear alpha and visible margins
passed their focused checks. Full heads, hands, hems and both feet fit within each
native canvas. The review sheets show both light settings at desktop width and
in a 390 by 580 phone view. Approved paintings provide the review light settings.
A separate identity sheet compares the approved Aulis face with its chapter portrait.

This art draft targets `codex/greek-house-of-blood`. Chapter integration owns final
actor bindings, left-facing metadata, optimized images, public assets, shared
rules and full release checks. Separate story items keep their chapter identities.

The first native Athena image remains in `sources/athena-initial.png`. Its
complete prompt and hashes are recorded in the generation history. The final
`athena.png` gives the whole helmet crest clear margin above the figure.
