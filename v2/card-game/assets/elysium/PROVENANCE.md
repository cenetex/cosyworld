# Elysium: card art

Every image here comes from the Elysium art proposal in
`docs/art-direction/worldpacks/review-trios/`. Those images were generated
with the built-in image tool. Their exact prompts are in `builtin-receipts.json`
in that folder, and they are still marked as pending approval there. The
repository's existing [MIT license](../../../../LICENSE) applies.

| File                       | Source                           | Treatment                                                                                        |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `void-001.png`             | `elysium-location.png`           | Copied unchanged (1672 × 941).                                                                   |
| `gold-spiral.png`          | `elysium-location.png`           | The right 912 × 513 region, scaled to 1672 × 941.                                                |
| `keystone-ledge.png`       | `references/elysium-concept.png` | The board's main scene, with the figure painted out, cropped to 16:9 and scaled to 1672 × 941.   |
| `keystone-ledge-empty.png` | `keystone-ledge.png`             | The same scene with the obsidian stone painted out, leaving its plinth.                          |
| `resident.png`             | `elysium-avatar.png`             | Black paper keyed to transparency, set on a 1024 × 1536 RGBA canvas.                             |
| `wayfinder.png`            | `elysium-item.png`               | Black paper keyed to transparency, framed on a 1254 × 1254 RGBA canvas.                          |
| `keystone.png`             | `references/elysium-concept.png` | The board's obsidian stone study, keyed to transparency and framed on a 1254 × 1254 RGBA canvas. |

The Gold Spiral, Keystone Ledge and keystone images are cut from larger
images, so they are softer than the other packs' art. The Gold Spiral has no
ground, so travelers there stand along its lowest filament. If you replace
any image with a dedicated generation, keep the file name.
