Privacy

  • Who: Cenetex Inc. (Ontario, Canada) runs it. The RATi Open Software Foundation designed it.
  • Passkey: your device keeps the key. We keep its public half and an account number.
  • Signed in: we keep your avatars, shared worlds and their play history.
  • AI: AI providers, through OpenRouter, voice the characters from the game's own state. Nothing you type is sent, because there is no typing.
  • Play counts: anonymous daily totals. Turn them off in Settings. We honour Global Privacy Control.
  • Where: servers in California, logs with AWS in the USA, AI providers worldwide.
  • Contact: for questions or to delete your data, write to privacy@cenetex.com (email us).
  • Without us: download it and run it yourself from lonelyforest.com/download.

Who runs this, and which law applies

Designed by the RATi Open Software Foundation, in British Columbia, Canada. Operated as a service by Cenetex Inc. All revenue goes to the Foundation; Cenetex does not earn money from Lonely Forest. Cenetex Inc. is responsible for the service and the data this page describes, under the law of Ontario, Canada, including Canada's federal privacy law (PIPEDA).

Playing without signing in

Your game is saved in your browser only. Character lines are asked for through our server, which asks an AI model. The request holds facts from the game, such as places, characters and what just happened. It holds no text from you and no account.

Passkeys and your account

We collect your passkey so you can log in and keep your avatars on our shared servers, which use our shared AI on our domain. Your device keeps the private key, and there is no password. We store the passkey's public half, a random account number, a generated name, and when the passkey was made and last used. A sign-in stays valid for 30 days.

Shared worlds

Each shared world keeps an ordered record of every play and every spoken line, and which account holds which avatar. Other players see avatar names, never your account or passkey. A shared world keeps its spoken voice clips. A closed world keeps its record so it can be replayed.

AI providers

Character dialogue and voices are made by AI providers. We reach them through OpenRouter, which routes each request to a model maker such as OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, xAI or Mistral. Some routes promise not to keep requests; others follow the provider's own policy. Requests are built from the game's own state. Nothing you type is sent, because the game has no typing.

Play counts

We count plays without knowing who you are, to see how the table plays. The game counts things like how many times each kind of card is played, Talk included; Sleep and Wait; quests chosen, dropped and met; journeys and defeats; lines skipped or finished early; looks back at earlier lines; how long Thinking takes before a line, and when a line fails; whether the opening title was skipped or woken; and errors, by a short code. It sends only daily totals, about once a minute and when you leave.

The counts carry no account, world, device or IP address, no dialogue and no other text. We keep only each day's totals, for 400 days. Server logs never hold the counts. The counts never change your game: nothing is rewarded or chosen from them.

To stop them, turn off "Share anonymous play counts" in Settings. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted. The desktop app counts nothing unless you turn it on.

Logs

Server logs hold errors and timing. They may include a short piece of an AI-written line that the game refused. They are kept for 30 days in Amazon Web Services in the United States. Our host, Fly.io, sees your IP address to deliver the site. We use it in memory to limit request rates and don't store it.

Where data is processed

Our servers run on Fly.io in California, USA. Logs are kept with Amazon Web Services in the USA. AI requests go through OpenRouter to AI providers around the world, which may process them in other countries. Cenetex Inc. is based in Ontario, Canada.

Play on your own computer

If you want to play the game without sharing your passkey data with us, download, modify, and play the game yourself for free from lonelyforest.com/download.

Deleting your data

To clear a game you play without signing in, clear this site's data in your browser. There is no button to delete an account yet. To ask us to delete yours, write to privacy@cenetex.com. Shared worlds keep their play record so they can be replayed.

Children

Lonely Forest is for players aged 13 and older.
