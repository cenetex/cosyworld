Privacy

Playing without signing in

Your game is saved in your browser only. Character lines are asked for through our server, which asks an AI model. The request holds facts from the game, such as places, characters and what just happened. It holds no text from you and no account.

Passkeys and your account

We collect your passkey so you can log in and keep your avatars on our shared servers, which use our shared AI on our domain. Your device keeps the private key, and there is no password. We store the passkey's public half, a random account number, a generated name, and when the passkey was made and last used. A sign-in stays valid for 30 days.

Shared worlds

Each shared world keeps an ordered record of every play and every spoken line, and which account holds which avatar. Other players see avatar names, never your account or passkey. A closed world keeps its record so it can be replayed.

Credits

Credits pay for spoken voices and scene videos. Credit history is kept as a permanent ledger, and spoken voice clips are stored with their world. Credits can't be bought yet. When they can, a payment processor will take your card details itself; we will send it only your account number and the number of credits.

AI providers

Character dialogue, voices and videos are made by AI providers. We reach them through OpenRouter, which routes each request to a model maker such as OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, xAI or Mistral. Some routes promise not to keep requests; others follow the provider's own policy. Requests are built from the game's own state. Nothing you type is sent, because the game has no typing.

Gameplay counts

We don't collect gameplay counts. This page will say what we count before we start.

Logs

Server logs hold errors and timing. They may include a short piece of an AI-written line that the game refused. They are kept for 30 days in Amazon Web Services in the United States. Our host, Fly.io, sees your IP address to deliver the site. We use it in memory to limit request rates and don't store it.

Play on your own computer

If you want to play the game without sharing your passkey data with us, download, modify, and play the game yourself for free from lonelyforest.com/download.

Deleting your data

To clear a game you play without signing in, clear this site's data in your browser. There is no button to delete an account yet. To ask us to delete yours, write to [owner to fill: contact address]. The credit ledger is permanent, and shared worlds keep their play record so they can be replayed.

Children

Lonely Forest is for players aged 13 and older.
