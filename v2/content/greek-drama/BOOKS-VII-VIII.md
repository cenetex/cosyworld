# Sky, poets and the return table

The Sky invitation at Dione’s courtyard opens Cyclops, Birds, Peace,
Prometheus and the poets’ court. The eastern road and ship landing have
shared access flags. Each earlier Trojan and Persian cast keeps its own
account flag.

Cyclops uses separate food baskets, the actual cup and timber, and the
sailors’ and satyrs’ own consent and arrivals. Birds uses a real gate key,
food and a refuge tablet. Peace uses the harness, rope, grain and tools.
Prometheus uses the ordinary lamp and its finite oil, Io’s received message,
and the terms carried to Hermes.

Frogs adds Xanthias at the lower landing and Aeschylus and Euripides at the
poets’ court. A traveler can carry the bag into court or give it to
Xanthias. He keeps that accepted burden. The traveler sets the scales beside
a reading. After the final page, a listener who heard the whole reading can
choose Aeschylus, Euripides or the living chorus for the return tablet.
This choice uses its own saved key.

Each journey has a final-account Talk topic that names its actual outcome
and directs the record’s carrier to Dione. Another human can collect that
record, hear its account, and carry it home. The escape list, refuge tablet,
peace list, storm record and reading tablet each support a homecoming reading.

## Reading and review

A start lays down the actual material and freezes the earned poem through
the preceding turn. Each page has four stanzas. Each stanza keeps its
original receipt turn and author. The reader has a separate identity.
The last required page earns the site’s completion and its new verse.

Each human has one reserved reading at each of six sites. Eight later
readings share a separate allowance. The archive keeps compact tuples and
fits its 384 KiB bound. The original reader can review the frozen planned
pages. Other listeners can review the pages they heard. Menu → Shared
readings opens this review.

An invited guest who leaves the room or disconnects keeps that departure
for later automatic pages. Their return allows an explicit replay to that
original listener. Required listeners can return before the next page.
Replay keeps the original reading turn, cutoff and page publication turn.
It adds the listener’s hearing receipt.

Dialogue uses a page only within its actual audience. Later room talk uses
only pages heard by everyone in the current active company. Saved private
speech and memories keep that same scope. The host checks the exact page
against its published catalog. The local model counts its real tokens and
keeps input within 1,408 tokens and output within 192 tokens. It can split
its own input while the game keeps the same four-stanza page.

## Source frame

Aristophanes’s [Frogs](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristophanes/frogs.html)
supplies the bag, descent, poetic contest and source choice of Aeschylus.
The playable tablet return, Euripides choice, living chorus choice and all
couplets are original CosyWorld adaptations. The source choice and the
playable alternatives keep their own names.
