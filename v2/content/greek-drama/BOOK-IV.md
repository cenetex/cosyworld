# The Faces of Home

Book IV adds five named accounts: Helen, Andromache, Hippolytus, Medea and Ion.
Myrto offers them after the Open Door return. Each chapter has three played
beats, a chosen result and a carried return for Dione. The source is
`card-world.json`. `book-iv-ids.json` lists every added identity.

This is part of the combined version 1.2.5, rules 21 and Greek revision 8
batch. The new source adds 23 actors, 30 items, 12 places, 112 choices,
64 spoken topics, 20 story cards and 36 stanza definitions. Each played
route earns four couplets. Each outcome has its own signed return action.
The 56 earlier stanza definitions retain their wording and order.

## Helen

Helen’s existing Egypt and Troy choices keep their accepted records. The
Egyptian chapter compares Helen’s words with the shipwreck messenger’s
evidence. A carried appeal brings Theonoe’s own answer. The traveller can
prepare an empty funeral voyage, seek a public hearing, or support Helen’s
chosen sanctuary. Helen gives her own assent to each public or travel plan.

The escape needs both travellers’ recorded consent, the actual prepared
cloth and provisions, Menelaus at the local boat, and Helen there with her
witness tablet. Its final report names the Egyptian crew’s deaths.
The signed result then travels to Dione through its actual holder.
The shared Greek ship serves `greek-book-iv-helen-landing`.
The funeral boat is a local place in this account.

[Complete Helen text](https://www-current.chs.harvard.edu/primary-source/euripides-helen/).
The public hearing and continuing sanctuary are CosyWorld alternatives.

## Andromache

The Phthian household belongs after Troy. Molossus is Andromache’s son with
Neoptolemus. Astyanax is her son with Hector in the separate Trojan account.
The named family reports keep those two children clear.

Food reaches the shrine. Peleus answers a real summons and reaches the
shrine through Accompany. Mother and child each agree to their own journey.
Each reaches the protected household before a chosen stay or boundary
road. The onward road needs Andromache’s further assent. Their arrival
reports record their actual saved places. The Delphic messenger’s separate
account records Neoptolemus’s death in both routes. Thetis’s later settlement
stays a named source report. Andromache authorises the carried result.

[Complete Andromache text](https://classics.mit.edu/Euripides/andromache.html).
The independent boundary departure is a CosyWorld alternative.

## Hippolytus

This is the Troezen household account. Theseus’s answers arrive through its
household messenger. Phaedra and her nurse speak in their own moments.
Water and a cooling cloth reach the sickroom. The nurse’s death report
precedes the sealed accusation. Hippolytus’s oath and the nurse’s witness
have separate published words. Both papers reach the evidence table.

The hearing route needs a delivered delay request and Theseus’s reported
consent. The source route has separate curse, injury and Artemis reports.
The covering cloth reaches the injured son. His forgiveness precedes the
final death report. The nurse’s paper carries the chosen result home.

[Hippolytus text](https://classics.mit.edu/Euripides/hippolytus.html) and
[complete final scene](https://www-current.chs.harvard.edu/primary-source/euripides-hippolytus-sb/).
The hearing before punishment is a CosyWorld alternative.

## Medea

The Corinthian household belongs before Aegeus’s return to Athens.
Creon of Corinth has his own identity. The nurse and attendant name Medea
and Jason’s elder and younger sons as two family members.

Food and an actual one-day grant earn the first beat. Aegeus’s oath reaches
Medea as a carried tablet. Its promised refuge begins after arrival in
Athens. The local road out of Corinth has its own deeds.

Medea discloses the dangerous gifts before the nurse examines their actual
robe and crown. In the living route, Medea and the nurse give separate
consent and each reaches the quay beyond the gate. Placed road supplies
and the attendant’s report establish both sons’ safety. Athens remains an
onward duty. The source route needs gift delivery, separate royal and child
death reports, and a memorial account. The public route needs exposed gifts,
the attendant’s guarded shelter report and Creon’s exile decision.
The signed return keeps the chosen result clear.

[Complete Medea text](https://classics.mit.edu/Euripides/medea.html).
Living departure and guarded public reckoning are CosyWorld alternatives.

## Ion

This is the earlier Delphic foundation tale. Ion works beside the priestess
who raised him. The broom and water return to their actual temple places.
Ion reports the failed poisoning and the old servant’s exposed part.
The player then secures the dangerous cup and hears Creusa before judgment.

Creusa names the woven cloth, golden snakes and olive wreath before the
priestess entrusts the real basket and signs. Two carried loads bring all
four objects to the temple. Creusa agrees to come there. The recognition
report needs her actual arrival and every laid object.

Maternal recognition and Athena’s closing instruction have separate saved
scene records. The latter belongs to the local mother-and-son scene.
Xuthus stays at the guest house with his own public household account.
The private route shares an approved care and chosen-family excerpt.
Creusa gives her own consent to the public maternal route. Ion chooses
his own temple household in the third route. Dione receives the authorised
care excerpt. The Bard sings only the earned couplets.

[Complete Ion text](<https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Plays_of_Euripides_(Coleridge)/Ion>).
Public maternal testimony and the lasting temple household are CosyWorld
alternatives.
