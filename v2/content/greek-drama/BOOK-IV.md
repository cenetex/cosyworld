# The Faces of Home

Book IV adds five named accounts: Helen, Andromache, Hippolytus, Medea and Ion.
This branch is an unreleased rules-21 batch on the capacity foundation.
The package remains 1.2.5 and the Greek revision is 8.

## Helen

Myrto offers the five accounts after the Open Door return. Helen’s existing
Egypt and Troy choices keep their accepted records. The new Egyptian chapter
compares Helen’s words with the shipwreck messenger’s evidence. A carried
appeal brings Theonoe’s own answer. The traveller can prepare an empty funeral
voyage, seek a public hearing, or support Helen’s chosen sanctuary.

The escape requires both travellers’ recorded consent, prepared supplies,
Menelaus at the local boat, and Helen there with her witness tablet.
The escape report names the Egyptian crew’s deaths. A signed account then
returns to Dione through its actual holder. The ordinary shared ship stays
separate from the local funeral boat.

The approved Helen paintings and Theonoe and Theoclymenus portraits come
from the art work recorded in `assets/greek-drama/HELEN-ART.md`.
The remaining portraits and objects use the existing Greek art collection.
