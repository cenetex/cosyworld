# Greek ship journeys

The rules-21 epic draft uses one shared ship and eleven landings. Its saved
dock, destination and recall describe that same vessel for every player and
resident. The first passenger ashore sets the arrival for everyone aboard.
An empty ship makes its circuit when a player waits at a landing.

| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Black Pond          | The forest shore                            |

Each landing has a local path to its nearby scene. Roads with fixed ends join
towns. The Trojan landing opens through the Ajax invitation or Helen’s Trojan
account. The Egyptian landing follows the selected Egyptian account. Each
later chapter opens its own shore through its invitation. Full-access saved
worlds retain their wider map.

Helen’s local funeral boat is a separate story scene beside the Egyptian
landing. Its loading and escort belong to that episode. The shared ship
carries the actual voyage between the eleven landings.

## Resident voyages

A resident can use a friendly companion’s item at their shared meeting.
Boarding for another meeting place needs custody of that item. A resident
can follow its owner when the owner boards through an actual travel play.

A resident already aboard can finish the saved leg through a legal arrival
when its larger goal becomes blocked. Human passengers choose the port first.
The empty circuit begins after all actual passengers step ashore. Each circuit
wait checks the vessel’s real passengers and restores the resulting save.

## Earlier shore visits

Rules 20 and Greek revision 7 had direct distant links at Egypt, Helen’s
Trojan beach, Tauris, Sicily, Aulis, Lemnos, the Trojan camp and Thrace. The
upgrade expands their learned links into the corresponding local paths and
shared-ship edges. It preserves old log entries, belongings, positions,
accounts and the ship’s actual position. Earlier visitors gain the ship’s
known crossing so they can wait for it at their shore and sail home.

The continuation tests first make and validate the old move with the frozen
rules-20 interpreter. They then load that actual save, reach the local
landing, wait for the vessel, board, sail home and reload. A separate case
uses Mara’s saved visit. The empty-circuit case commits each wait through
the ordinary rules and restores every step from its save.
