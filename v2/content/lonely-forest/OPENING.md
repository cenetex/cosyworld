# Tea beneath the oak

The first card chapter has four places: Cosy Cottage, Rain-Soft Garden,
Moonlit Trail and Old Oak Tree. RATi speaks in words. Skull answers through
physical movement. Whiskerwind speaks in two to five emojis. Oak speaks
through Root, Ring, Leaf and Hollow.

The creator premise comes from `docs/art-direction/README.md`. The old oak
is the waking body of the Tree of Dreams. RATi, Skull and Whiskerwind are
its three walking personalities. Each has a distinct manner. This chapter
reveals their link through a familiar detail repeated at the oak.

## The journey

1. Meet RATi in the cottage. A personal Story Button appears somewhere
   reachable beyond the cottage. Each traveler has their own button.
2. Find the button and give it to RATi. A friend can carry or deliver it.
   RATi sews it into a scarf and gives the scarf to its owner. The button
   stays attached to that scarf. Choose **Stay for tea** beside her.
3. In the garden, choose **Clear the garden drain** or **Set the stepping
   stones**. Each choice opens the Moonlit Trail. The chosen work stays in
   the world history.
4. Take the Dewbright Button. Beside Whiskerwind, choose **Set the button
   on a leaf**. Whiskerwind shares rain, pawprints, home and tea.
5. Visit Skull on the trail. Choose **Set the bell beside Skull** while
   carrying the Watch Bell, or choose **Mark the pawprints**. Each gives
   the watch a saved form. Take the Wolfprint Charm.
6. At Oak, choose **Tell Oak about the hearth** or **Tell Oak about the
   path**. The scarf, symbols and watch give the account its facts. Hollow
   repeats a detail from the journey.
7. Return to RATi with the charm. Choose **Leave the charm by the hearth**
   or **Keep the charm for the next journey**. A warm cup closes the visit.

The two path choices, two watch choices, two oak accounts and two charm
endings each keep their own result. A later visitor meets the completed
work. Cooperation uses the shared world's normal custody and action rules.

## Story cards

Each traveler gets a personal scarf card after meeting RATi. The card follows
that traveler's button and shows when their scarf is ready. The finished card
and the scarf item share that traveler's scarf illustration. The first personal
scarf adds a shared hearth story for Oak. Earlier saved blue-scarf stories keep
their original records.

The chapter also keeps these shared card lines:
- **Footsteps through the rain.** appears after the tea invitation and ends
  with the shared symbols at the repaired path.
- **What will the oak hear?** appears after helping Skull and ends with the
  chosen charm homecoming.

Saved choices and receipts own the shared results. Personal meeting and
sewing records keep each traveler's result. The shared story registry keeps actual participants and
the completion turn through reload and trimmed conversation history.

## Mirror crossing

The Black cloth physically in the attic opens entry to the cottage. The
cloth may lie on the attic floor, be carried by someone in the attic, or
cover an object physically there. The `item_present` descriptor follows
nested custody. Flame state and fuel are separate facts.

The cottage keeps an unconditional return to the attic. Taking the cloth
through the mirror closes entry from the attic until the cloth returns.
The cottage's return remains available through that change.
