# Hoppycat: February Third

Every dawn is February Second. The forest can reach its next day only by
building a continuity honest enough to change: sourced but not frozen,
imaginative but not misleading, memorable but bounded, and never forced past a
requested pause.

This is an original cosy-weird fantasy world synthesized from recurring themes
across HoppyCat's public work. It is not a repository tour. Repositories remain
outside the fiction as provenance and design inputs.

The authored pack includes:

- 10 connected locations, from the Halfway Tea Garden to the Signal Jetty;
- 9 original residents—including Hoppy Cat—and 3 player-avatar forms, origins, and callings;
- 10 portable story items with concrete quest uses;
- 3 factions organized around record, rendering, and careful circulation;
- 3 playable quests, 6 progress/danger clocks, and 3 active fronts;
- 19 generated-art cards for locations and moving residents, plus 10 pending-art item cards.

Every authored resident has ambient autonomy and explicit roaming enabled. They
can pursue useful goals first, then choose a legal adjacent route when idle;
recent-route checks keep them from simply bouncing back and forth. Player
avatars move through the same routes under player control.

The primary quest is **Ring in February Third**. Players inspect a corrected
record, plant one bounded context seed, choose an unfinished prism's final
facet, restore the bell's authority to pause, and tie the chosen red thread.

The GitHub observation index in `v2/worlds/hoppycat/github-index.json` is still
non-authoritative. It tells curators when the public source landscape changes;
it cannot add a room, actor, item, quest fact, or journal event automatically.

See `docs/worldpacks/hoppycat-living-archive.md` for the full world design,
avatar model, gameplay loop, source boundaries, sync-agent roles, and proposed
deployment sequence for `hoppycat.lonelyforest.com`.
