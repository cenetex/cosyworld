# Hoppycat Card Art Direction

All 29 card images are fixed local worldpack assets. The ten item images were
generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool, reviewed as a set, resized to
1024 by 1024, and stored as WebP files. They must not be regenerated at runtime.

## Locked style references

- `hoppycat-hoppy-cat.webp`: avatar palette, character rendering, and finish.
- `hoppycat-halfway-tea-garden.webp`: forest, lantern, red-thread, and star-map motifs.
- `hoppycat-prism-foundry.webp`: glass, brass, jewel colors, and workshop materials.
- `hoppycat-red-thread-spool.webp`: approved square item composition anchor.

## Shared item prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Hoppycat game item card art, square
image. Closely match the locked references: richly detailed painterly
anime-storybook fantasy illustration, tactile handcrafted materials,
jewel-toned cobalt and forest-green shadows, warm amber lantern highlights,
fine hand-painted texture, magical but grounded. Center exactly one complete
item as a magical still life. The item fills about 70 percent of the square
frame in three-quarter view with a clear silhouette and generous padding. Use a
simple mossy stone-and-wood surface and a softly vignetted forest-workshop
backdrop with subtle star-map glints, not a full location scene. Light it with
an intimate golden lantern glow against deep blue-green dusk. No people,
animals, faces, eyes, hands, characters, readable text, letters, numbers,
logos, watermark, UI, card border, trading-card frame, duplicate item, loose
unrelated objects, or copied composition from the references.
