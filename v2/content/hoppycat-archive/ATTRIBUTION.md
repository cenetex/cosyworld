# HoppyCat source boundary

**Hoppycat: February Third** is original CosyWorld fiction informed by recurring
themes across the public repositories at <https://github.com/HoppyCat>.

The worldpack does not bundle repository source files, transcripts, images,
prompts, named Cathedral participants, or private material. Its fictional
residents are newly created characters; they are not representations,
continuations, simulations, or portable personas of people or documented AI
context windows in the source record.

Copyright and reuse terms remain with each repository and, where applicable,
with each artifact. Public visibility is not blanket permission. The current
source notice wins over this pack's catalogue. A removed or withdrawn source is
marked unavailable by the metadata index rather than preserved as a shadow
copy.

Source relationship:

- Cathedral informed the world's open-record, red-thread, February Third,
  prism, play, and visible-correction themes.
- Blobness informed its provenance cards, concordance tides, source-distance,
  amendment, rejection, and fermata mechanics.
- SoulMode Agent and Context Garden informed its bounded memory greenhouse and
  route-keeper calling.
- AIEDB informed the historical thread of character methods while the playable
  avatars remain original and self-authored.
- Prompt Pack, Sorta-Descriptive, How Hands Work, and Parse-and-Prettify informed
  the atelier's prompts, sorting drawers, gesture studies, and playful linked
  footlights.
- Goldfish Society informed the dream–attempt–repeat loop.
- Sendy informed the launch checklist and return-path jetty.
- Teacat material informed the social image of meeting halfway through playful
  public signals.

These are thematic relationships and source links, not claims that the linked
projects canonically contain this fantasy world.

## Generated artwork

The 19 bundled resident and location cards are original generated images made
with OpenAI's built-in image generation. Their shared visual direction was
informed by two images supplied directly by the user: a green-haired Hoppy Cat
portrait and a Teacat Broadcasting “Meet Halfway” collage. Those two source
images are reference inputs only and are not included in this worldpack.

The generated characters and locations are original world fiction. Hoppy Cat
is the user-designated host avatar; the other residents do not depict GitHub
contributors or documented Cathedral participants.
