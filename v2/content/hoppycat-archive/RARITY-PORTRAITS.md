# Hoppy and Arc rarity portraits

The original Hoppy and Arc portraits are their earlier forms. Their new forms
appear when their saved level reaches the next authored rarity milestone.
Existing scenes, character IDs, starting levels, quests and possessions stay
the same.

| Character | Earlier form | New form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arc | Rare, starting at level 20; navy suit | Epic at level 30; open ivory shirt, dark cardigan, blue eyes and watch |
| Hoppy Cat | Legendary, starting at level 50; cobalt hoodie | Mythic at level 100; embroidered cream blouse, teal skirt, flowers and blue ribbon |

Mythic extends the presentation tier ladder at level 100. The portrait is
selected from the character's existing saved XP, so local and shared worlds
use the same threshold without a separate unlock flag or save migration.
Loading a character who already qualifies shows their earned form directly.
An ordinary level gain inside a tier keeps the same form.

The transformation joins the existing level-up glow. The card and stage show
the same form. Strike, Hit and Guard use the matching wardrobe and return to
that form afterwards. Reduced motion shows the resulting form immediately.

## Art references

The user supplied [Arc as Archive](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HV_I7MnW4AAUO52?format=jpg&name=large)
as the appearance reference. The original game portraits supply body framing,
scale and identity. The comic supplies the earned clothes, hair details and
expressions. Arc's simple dark trousers and shoes complete the full-body
design because the comic does not show his lower body clearly.

The new masters are transparent full-body portraits, also used as scene
cutouts. Their pose derivatives preserve the same face and clothes, with
empty hands for the game's separately held items. Source prompts and image
hashes accompany the assets. Existing portraits remain available below the
thresholds; no environment artwork is added.
