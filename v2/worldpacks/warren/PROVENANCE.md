# Art provenance

Every asset in `assets/` was made with the built-in image tool in the CosyWorld
house style (painted animation art, clear shapes, subtle ink contours, indigo
shadows and worn amber highlights). Backdrops are opaque 1024 × 576 PNG;
character and item art was generated on a flat magenta field, keyed to a
transparent background, and despilled.

## threshold.png

File: [threshold.png](assets/threshold.png). Transparent background: false.

Prompt:

> Painted animation art background scenery for a whimsical card RPG, clear shapes, subtle ink contours, indigo shadows and worn amber highlights. Strange and atmospheric, concrete ordinary objects, restrained details. Wide 16:9 pure environment background ONLY — absolutely no dialogue boxes, no text boxes, no UI frames, no borders, no panels: the bottom of the frame is ordinary scene floor. The mouth of a small earthen burrow beneath a dark pond, reached through a long crack in wet grey stone. Thin silvery light through the crack. On the stone lip, small lost objects arranged with care: a button, a bendy straw, a small brass key. Moss, damp stone, faint warm glow from inside the burrow. Clear spacious foreground. No characters, no lettering.
